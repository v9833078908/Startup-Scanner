# Investment Thesis — iFree Scouting Pipeline

## Who We Are
iFree — tech company (not a fund) launching investment activity and searching for ideas for own products.

## Investment Parameters
- **Check size:** $30K — $300K per deal
- **Stage:** seed / early growth
- **Funnel:** ~30 startups → 1–2 investments
- **Geography:** Russia + Global
- **Key filter:** Strong founders — team matters more than idea

## Focus Sectors
- AI / ML
- Fintech
- GameDev
- Developer Tools
- Infrastructure
- Automation

## Two Operating Modes

### Invest Mode
Find startups to invest in. Key questions:
- How strong is the founder?
- Is there traction?
- Does our check size fit ($30–300K)?
- Can iFree add value (contacts, audience, cross-sell)?
- What's the exit potential?

### Build Mode
Find ideas for own products. Patterns:
1. **YC trends → Russian adaptation** — YC invests in N startups in category X, no Russian analogue
2. **Open-source with stars but no business** — 10K+ stars, active dev, no company/revenue
3. **Hot product + iFree audience = cross-sell** — new B2B product + iFree's clients
4. **Hot niche, no dominant player** — multiple small startups, growing market, no leader

## Three Deal Flow Streams
1. **Automated scouting** — parser pipeline (this system)
2. **Top-down** — dealflow exchange with funds and syndicates
3. **Warm intros** — trusted personal connections (bonus in scoring)

## Scoring Summary
- **Invest:** 10 criteria, weighted (see config/scoring_weights.yaml). INVEST ≥8 / WATCH 6–7.9 / PASS <6
- **Build:** 8 criteria, weighted. BUILD ≥8 / PARTNER 6–7.9 / MONITOR 4–5.9 / SKIP <4
